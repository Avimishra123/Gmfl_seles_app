# Privacy Policy for GMFL Salesperson

**Last Updated:** October 22, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 22, 2026  

Welcome to **GMFL Salesperson** ("App"), operated and maintained by **GMFL** ("Company", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and related financial sales management services.

By accessing or using the GMFL Salesperson application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide our loan management and sales coordination services, we collect the following categories of information:

### A. Sales Partner / Agent Information
- **Personal Details:** Full name, contact phone number, email address, sales partner code/agent ID, and profile image (optional).
- **Authentication Data:** Login credentials, security tokens, and OTP verifications.

### B. Lead & Applicant Information
When sales representatives input customer details or assist applicants in submitting loan requests, we collect:
- **Applicant Personal Information:** Name, mobile number, email address, residential address, and employment details.
- **Loan Details:** Requested loan type (Home Loan, Personal Loan, Business Loan, etc.), required loan amount, and proposed tenure.

### C. Documentation & Verification Data (KYC)
For the appraisal and processing of credit facilities, sales agents upload or verify applicant documents, including:
- Proof of Identity (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport).
- Proof of Income & Banking (Salary slips, Form 16, Bank account statements).
- Property or business registration papers (where applicable).

### D. Device and Technical Information
- **Device Details:** Device manufacturer, model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and network information.
- **App Analytics & Logs:** App performance metrics, crash logs, access timestamps, and interactions.
- **Push Notification Tokens:** Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to deliver real-time alerts regarding application status changes.

---

## 2. Permissions Requested by the App

The App requests the following Android permissions strictly for operational functionality:
- **INTERNET (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Required to communicate with our secure backend APIs and cloud services.
- **POST NOTIFICATIONS (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Required to deliver real-time push alerts on application approvals, document status changes, and announcements.
- **STORAGE & MEDIA ACCESS / DOCUMENT PICKER:** Required to allow agents to select and upload necessary KYC and financial documents from the device.
- **VIBRATE & WAKE LOCK (`android.permission.VIBRATE`, `android.permission.WAKE_LOCK`):** Required for notification alert sounds and timely delivery.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We process collected information for the following legitimate business purposes:
1. **Loan Processing & Underwriting:** Facilitating the review, appraisal, and sanctioning of loan applications submitted by sales partners.
2. **Sales Tracking & Commission Management:** Attributing leads and completed loan disbursements to the respective sales representative.
3. **Document Verification & Compliance:** Ensuring adherence to statutory Know Your Customer (KYC), Anti-Money Laundering (AML), and regulatory guidelines.
4. **Communication & Customer Support:** Providing operational status updates, service notifications, and resolving queries.
5. **Security & Fraud Prevention:** Detecting, preventing, and addressing unauthorized access, fraudulent loan submissions, and technical glitches.

---

## 4. How We Share and Disclose Information

We value your trust and do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or customer data to third parties for commercial advertising. Information may be shared only under the following circumstances:

- **Financial Partners & Lenders:** Relevant applicant and document data are shared with authorized NBFCs, banks, and lending partners involved in underwriting and disbursing the credit facility.
- **Service Providers:** Trusted third-party technology providers (such as Google Firebase for cloud notifications and secure cloud storage providers) that assist us under strict non-disclosure and data privacy agreements.
- **Legal & Regulatory Authorities:** When required by applicable laws, court orders, or governmental directives to comply with statutory legal processes.

---

## 5. Data Security & Storage

We implement rigorous technical and organizational security measures to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:
- All data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard **TLS/SSL encryption**.
- Data at rest is safeguarded using restricted access controls and modern encryption standards.
- Access to applicant KYC documents is restricted strictly to authorized verification personnel and underwriting teams.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal and loan-related data only as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes outlined in this policy, or as mandated by applicable regulatory and financial retention guidelines.

Sales partners or applicants wishing to request the deletion or correction of their personal data may contact our Data Privacy Officer at the contact details provided below. Upon verification, data that is not legally required to be retained will be securely erased.

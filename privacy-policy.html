Privacy Policy for GMFL Salesperson

Effective Date: October 22, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 22, 2026
Welcome to GMFL Salesperson ("App"), operated and maintained by GMFL ("Company", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information when sales representatives and channel partners manage loan applications, leads, and document verification.

1. Information We Collect

To enable efficient loan lifecycle management and lead attribution, we collect the following types of information:

A. Sales Partner / Agent Data

  • Identification Details: Name, mobile phone number, email address, assigned agent code, and optional profile picture.
  • Authentication Information: One-time passwords (OTP) and session tokens to maintain authorized access.

B. Applicant & Lead Details

  • Personal Information: Customer name, contact number, email address, residential address, and employment details.
  • Application Parameters: Loan product type (Home Loan, Personal Loan, Business Loan), requested loan amount, and proposed tenure.

C. KYC & Loan Documentation

  • Government-issued Identity documents (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport).
  • Financial and Income proofs (Salary slips, Form 16, Bank statements).
  • Property and business records necessary for credit appraisal.

D. Technical & Device Data

  • Device identifiers, operating system version, and network connection status.
  • Push Notification Tokens (via Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver instant updates on application status changes.

2. App Permissions and Their Purpose

The GMFL Salesperson app requires the following standard Android permissions strictly to support core business functionality:

  • INTERNET: Needed to securely sync loan records, lead pipelines, and document statuses with our servers.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to notify agents about document approvals, under-review updates, and disbursements in real time.
  • FILE / STORAGE PICKER: Needed to allow field agents to upload applicant verification documents from their device storage.
  • VIBRATE & WAKE_LOCK: Used to signal incoming alerts and notifications reliably.

3. How We Use Collected Information

All data collected is used solely for legitimate business operations:

  • Facilitating the verification, appraisal, and processing of credit applications with lending institutions.
  • Tracking agent performance, target achievements, and lead incentives.
  • Complying with statutory Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) standards.
  • Communicating application status changes, pending requirements, and operational alerts.
  • Preventing fraud and securing system integrity.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We strictly protect your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or lease personal or customer data to third parties for marketing or advertising. Data is shared only with:

  • Regulated Lending Partners: Relevant financial institutions, banks, and NBFCs directly evaluating the loan application.
  • Essential Infrastructure Partners: Cloud hosting and Google Firebase messaging services operating under strict non-disclosure obligations.
  • Law Enforcement & Regulators: When strictly mandated by statutory legal obligations or regulatory compliance.

5. Data Security

We employ robust enterprise-grade safeguards to protect data from unauthorized access or breaches:

  • All data transfers are encrypted with industry-standard TLS/SSL protocols.
  • Stored documents and customer data are secured with strict role-based access controls.

6. Data Retention and Deletion Rights

We keep personal and transaction records only as long as needed to fulfill the business purposes outlined herein or as required by applicable financial record-keeping laws.

Users may request the review, update, or deletion of their personal information by contacting our privacy team. Legally mandated records will be handled in compliance with applicable statutory regulations.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is designed exclusively for adult sales executives and authorized business partners aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" timestamp.
